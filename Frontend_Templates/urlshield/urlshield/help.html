Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed answers about scanning, risk classification, detection features, privacy and project limitations.

How do I scan a link?

Open URL Analyzer, paste the full website address and press Analyze. You can also paste a link on the Dashboard and continue to the analyzer.

What do the three verdicts mean?
  • Legitimate / Safe (0–19): no meaningful warning signs detected.
  • Suspicious (20–59): one or more warning signs were detected.
  • Potential Phishing (60–100): the URL matches multiple common phishing patterns.
What features are checked?
  • HTTPS or plain HTTP
  • Raw IP addresses
  • “@” URL tricks
  • Punycode and encoded characters
  • URL length and complexity
  • Hyphen and digit patterns
  • Subdomain depth
  • Login, verification, account and KYC keywords
  • Risky top-level domains
  • Link-shortening services
  • Brand impersonation
  • Look-alike spellings such as paypa1 or micr0soft
  • Redirect parameters
Is the result 100% accurate?

No detector can guarantee perfect accuracy. URLShield is designed as a transparent security-analysis system. A sophisticated phishing website can use a clean-looking domain, while a legitimate website may have an unusual URL. Always treat the result as a security indicator rather than an absolute guarantee.

Why is domain age not shown?

Domain age, WHOIS information and live redirect chains require external server-side lookups. The browser-based version focuses on information available directly from the URL. A future backend can add domain registration and reputation intelligence.

Where is my scan history stored?

Scan history is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. It is not uploaded by this front-end demonstration. Visit the Scan History page to review or clear your records.

What is included in the dataset?

The project dataset contains labeled example URLs representing legitimate, suspicious and phishing-style cases. The phishing examples are created for demonstration and testing, not copied from active campaigns.

Can URLShield replace professional security tools?

No. URLShield is an academic and portfolio-oriented cybersecurity project. Production-grade detection requires continuously updated threat intelligence, DNS analysis, certificate intelligence, reputation feeds, sandboxing and model retraining.

🚨 Suspicious Website Response Guide

If URLShield identifies a website as suspicious, follow these general security practices.

1️⃣

Do not enter credentials

Never provide passwords, OTPs, banking details or recovery codes to an unverified website.

2️⃣

Verify independently

Open the official website manually instead of using the suspicious link received through email, SMS or social media.

3️⃣

Secure compromised accounts

If credentials were submitted, change the password through the official service and enable multi-factor authentication.
